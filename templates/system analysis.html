How It Works (For Everyone)

1. The Push (Force)

Pneumatics is just using **compressed air** to push things. Higher **Pressure (P)** means a stronger push. But if the **Mass (M)** is heavy, it takes longer to start moving.

2. The Resistance

**Damping (D)** is like moving your hand through water—it slows the piston down. **Spring (C)** is like a rubber band trying to pull the piston back to its home position.

3. The Brain (PID)

Without PID, the piston just flies out based on air pressure. With **PID enabled**, a computer "watches" the position and adjusts the air flow to stop perfectly at the **Red Target Line**.
